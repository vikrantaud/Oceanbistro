HOW TO PLAY
YOUR JOURNEY
• Each captain begins on their boat with a small floating restaurant. From the deck you dive for fish or visit the shop.
• In multiplayer you take turns: dive, serve your catch, then End Turn to pass the device to the next captain.

DIVING
• W A S D — swim. The reef is an open world: roam left/right into new waters, through caves and around rocks.
• SHIFT — sprint for a burst of speed (drains the stamina bar, then recovers).
• LEFT-CLICK — slash your melee weapon. Find new ones (katana, poison cutlass, rubber hammer…) in chests, switch & sharpen them in the shop.
• RIGHT-CLICK HOLD — raise your harpoon. Time slows while you aim. Release to fire.
• A hooked fish struggles — mash SPACE to reel it in before it escapes.
• 💨 Oxygen drains faster the deeper you go — grab the 🫧 oxygen tanks scattered on the seafloor to top up. Predators cost you health.
• ✚ Medical kits are scattered on the seafloor too — swim into one while hurt to restore 25% health.
• Swim back up to your boat any time to safely bank everything you caught.

THE RESTAURANT
• Back aboard, run the floor: WASD walk · SPACE grab a plate at the pass / serve a guest.
• At the drink bar, HOLD E to pour — stop at the red line. The liquid keeps flowing a beat after you release, so don't overfill!
• Tips raise your star rating. More stars = more companions who dive with you forever. Reach 10★ to Prestige for permanent boosts.
